Create a file named review_notes_[TIMESTAMP].md in the project root. We will
refer to this file as "notes".

To show that you understand the instructions, please print out a description
of how you understand them before you begin the review.

Conduct a thorough code review of the core, dispatch, desc and mcls
packages based on your research of them present in your context window.

For every line of inquiry, write an entry into notes that begins with a
classification between the following:

- No Concern
- Cosmetic
- Minor
- Major
- Critical

Then include an explanation of the finding, what it touches and what
recommendations you may have. If your recommendation includes multiple
options, label them clearly.

You must append findings to the notes file *incrementally* and on an ad hoc
basis. Write your first entry before moving deeper into a package.

Do not modify the project's source or tests.
